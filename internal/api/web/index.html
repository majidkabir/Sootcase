Changes

Defaults: 300 km and 30% from the EU Transport White Paper (2011), 100 km from the EU Combined Transport Directive (92/106/EEC). Road legs to and from the nearest rail terminals are added, and a terminal handling at each (GLEC v3.3 intermodal terminal value).
